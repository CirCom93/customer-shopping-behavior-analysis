# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using purchase data.

The goal is to understand sales performance, product trends, and customer segmentation in order to extract meaningful insights for business analysis.

The analysis explores different aspects of customer activity, including age group, gender, product categories, and seasonal trends, along with the distribution of sales and customer value. It also examines key factors such as total sales, number of orders, average review rating, and top customer behavior.

The project highlights the main relationships within the sales data, making it easier to identify the most important patterns in customer behavior and product performance.

The analysis and visualizations were created using SQL for data processing and Power BI for building the interactive dashboard.

## Tools Used

- SQL
- Power BI

## Dashboard Structure

### Page 1 - Overview / KPI Dashboard

This page provides a general overview of customer shopping behavior and sales performance.

It includes key metrics such as total customers, total sales, total orders, and average review rating.

It also includes filters for gender, category, and season, allowing dynamic exploration of the data.

The visualizations show sales by category and sales by season, helping to understand overall performance and seasonal trends.

---

### Page 2 - Customer Demographics

This page analyzes the composition of customers based on age group and gender.

It includes visualizations for customers by age group, sales by gender, and customers by age group and gender combined.

The goal is to understand how customers are distributed and how sales vary across different demographic segments.

---

### Page 3 - Product & Category Analysis

This page focuses on product performance and customer value.

It includes key metrics such as total customers, total sales, and average review rating.

It also shows sales by product and a table of top customers, including customer ID, total sales, total orders, and average review rating.

The goal is to identify the best-performing products and the most valuable customers based on purchase behavior.
